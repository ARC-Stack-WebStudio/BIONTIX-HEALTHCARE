Who We Are

Built for Modern Clinical Care. Backed by Engineering Precision.

BIONTIX HealthCare is a specialized healthcare infrastructure, medical equipment, and hospital furniture company headquartered in Thane, Maharashtra, serving hospitals and medical institutions across India.

Setting up or upgrading a healthcare facility often requires coordinating multiple disconnected vendors—one for modular construction and HVAC, another for clinical equipment, and yet another for hospital furniture. BIONTIX HealthCare bridges that gap.

Alongside turnkey execution of Modular Operation Theatres (OTs), ICUs, Isolation Rooms, Cleanrooms, and Medical HVAC systems, the company supplies high-durability hospital equipment and medical furniture engineered for daily clinical demands.

From initial layout planning and manufacturing to installation, testing, and commissioning, every solution is delivered with attention to hygiene, workflow efficiency, and applicable NABH and ISO cleanroom guidelines.
