How We Work

A Clear Roadmap for Every Healthcare Facility.

Whether you are building a modular Operation Theatre, setting up an ICU, or outfitting a facility with hospital equipment and medical furniture, precision starts long before installation begins. Our seven-stage execution model aligns clinical requirements, architectural layouts, commercial transparency, and on-site engineering—ensuring predictable timelines and dependable clinical performance.

  1. 01

    Initial Inquiry & Site Visit Scheduling

    Every project starts with a focused consultation to understand your requirements, whether OT/ICU infrastructure, HVAC, or hospital equipment. Our team coordinates with you to arrange a convenient site visit.

  2. 02

    Engineering Site Visit & Requirement Assessment

    Our engineers visit your facility to assess measurements, existing utilities, structural conditions, and clinical workflows. Based on the site assessment, we recommend practical engineering solutions tailored to your space.

  3. 03

    Existing & Proposed Layout Engineering

    We prepare an Existing Site Layout and a detailed Proposed Layout highlighting clinical zones, equipment placement, airflow routing, and furniture integration for your review and approval.

  4. 04

    Custom BOQ Design & Technical Confirmation

    After layout approval, we prepare a detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) covering equipment, systems, specifications, and installation requirements, aligned with your drawings and budget.

  5. 05

    Commercial Confirmation (PI & PO) & Project Mobilization

    Once the layout and BOQ are approved, we issue the Proforma Invoice (PI) and begin execution after receiving the Purchase Order (PO). Procurement and project mobilization follow.

  6. 06

    Qualified Site Supervision & Installation

    Our qualified site supervisor oversees installation, ensuring adherence to approved drawings, safety standards, workmanship, project timelines, and coordination with hospital teams.

  7. 07

    Final Inspection, Handover & Certified Quality

    All installed systems undergo final inspection and operational testing before handover. Products supplied by BIONTIX HealthCare are ISO and CE marked for recognized quality and safety standards.
